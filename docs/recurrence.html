06 / THE BOUNDARY OF THE CLAIM
Classical games.
Inspectable obligations.
The native solver records every nested round, every positional policy and every root outcome. A separate checker enumerates complete controller/environment policy pairs and simulates their unique plays. This browser independently does the same; it does not fetch native reports.
A safe target self-loop wins recurrence. A safe waiting loop without a target loses. The environment does not owe you an exit, even if that exit remains available. Goal outgoing moves are live, unlike terminal first-goal reach-avoid.
Deliberately bounded: at most six states, three moves per state and 729 assignments per player. Exact finite enumeration is not a scalable solver, a new theorem, a fairness assumption, cryptographic authenticity or a guarantee about missing real-world behavior.
